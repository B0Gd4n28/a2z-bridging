What Is a Bridging Loan?

Short-Term Finance, Built for Speed

A bridging loan is short-term finance secured against property, designed to bridge a gap. It is priced monthly (typically 0.55%–1.05%), completed in days rather than weeks, and repaid through a sale or refinance.

Chain Breaks & Fast Purchases

Buy your next property before the current one sells. A bridging loan covers the gap so you never lose the home you want to a slow chain.

Auction Deadlines

Won at auction? You have 28 days to complete or lose your deposit. Bridging finance is built for exactly this. Our fastest case completed in 48 hours.

Refurbishment & Capital Raise

Fund a light refurb before refinancing onto a term mortgage, or raise quick capital against property for a business opportunity.
